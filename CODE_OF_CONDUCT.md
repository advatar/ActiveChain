# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in ActiveChain a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Protect private, security-sensitive, and personally identifying information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Exploiting project spaces for spam, fraud, or unrelated commercial promotion.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a repository maintainer's GitHub contact channel. If the
report concerns that maintainer, contact another organization owner or GitHub Support. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, warnings,
temporarily or permanently restrict participation, or take other proportionate action.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, community spaces, and when an
individual officially represents the project.
